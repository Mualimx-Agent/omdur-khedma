Delete my data

If you have a published profile in Omdur Khedma, or you sent an application to be listed, you can ask for your data to be deleted at any time.

How to ask

Send a message to nazikmukhtar0@gmail.com and include:

  • the phone number shown in the profile, or the one you applied with
  • your application reference number, if you have one
  • what you want removed: the published profile, the application, or both

No account and no form is needed. A message from any address is enough.

What gets deleted

  • A published profile is removed from the app, together with the business name, phone number, description, districts and photos.
  • An application is deleted with all of its details and its photos.

How long it takes

The operator answers within 30 days and carries out the deletion within the same period.

What never leaves your phone

Favourites and the last search results that were loaded are stored on your device only; they never reach the operator. Clear them from the account page inside the app, or by uninstalling it.

What is not deleted on request

Reports that users send about profiles carry no name and no identifier of the person who sent them, so they cannot be traced back to anybody. They are deleted 12 months after they are closed.

The privacy notice has the full detail.
