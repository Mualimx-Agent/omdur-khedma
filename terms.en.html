What this app is

Omdur Khedma is a directory of local service providers in Omdurman. It shows you who offers a service and how to reach them.

It is not an employer, an agency, or a broker. It does not take part in the agreement you make with a provider, does not set prices, does not supervise the work, and does not handle any money.

What this app is not responsible for

The operator does not guarantee the quality, safety, punctuality, honesty, or legality of any work carried out by a listed provider. Any agreement is between you and the provider.

Provider details are supplied by the providers themselves. They may be incomplete, out of date, or wrong.

Verification badges

A badge on a profile means only what it says:

  • Not reviewed: the profile was submitted and nobody has checked it.
  • Phone confirmed: a reviewer reached the number and the person confirmed the profile.
  • Profile reviewed: a reviewer read the profile details and found them plausible.

No badge is a licence, a state check, a background check, an insurance, or a promise about the quality of any work.

Using the app as a customer

  • No account is needed.
  • Do not use the app to harass anybody or to send unsolicited advertising.
  • Report a profile only when there is a real reason to.

Being listed as a provider

By sending an application you confirm that:

  • the details you gave are true
  • you are entitled to offer the service you describe
  • you consent to your phone number being shown publicly in the app
  • the photos you uploaded are your own and show your own work

Sending an application does not entitle you to be listed. Applications are reviewed and may be rejected. A published profile may be suspended at any time, for example after credible reports.

Not allowed in a profile: links or web addresses, other people's photos, misleading claims about qualifications, and any content that is illegal.

Contacting providers

Calls and WhatsApp messages leave this app and are handled by your phone and by WhatsApp under their own terms. Nothing is sent by the app itself.

Changes and termination

The operator may change these terms, change the app, or stop offering it. A provider may ask for their profile to be removed at any time.

Misuse

The directory is not a way to harass people. Using the phone numbers published in the app for nuisance, extortion or fraud may be a criminal offence under the Law on Combating Cybercrimes 2018, quite apart from the profile being suspended.

Governing law

These terms are governed by the law of Sudan. The courts of Khartoum State have jurisdiction over any dispute arising from them, unless a mandatory rule provides otherwise.
